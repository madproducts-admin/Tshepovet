Part 1

System architecture and the waterfall API engine

Node.js/TypeScript, PostgreSQL 16 and Redis. Every channel (API, portal, WhatsApp, monitoring) calls the same verification services.

1.1Design goals, in priority order

  1. Unit margin at R3.00 per check. Every design choice is judged by its effect on the cost of a check: caching, request coalescing, prepaid bureau bundles, cost-aware routing, and not charging for our own outages.
  2. POPIA by construction. Tenant isolation is enforced in the database, identifiers are encrypted with blind indexes, the cache scope is decided by the data, and deletion is automatic.
  3. Correctness under failure. Holds instead of locks, a transactional outbox, idempotency keys, circuit breakers, and stale-if-error with explicit flags.
  4. One codebase for API, portal, WhatsApp and monitoring. Every channel calls the same verification services.

1.3Request lifecycle: POST /v1/verify/company

Some properties follow from this structure:

  • No charge without delivery. The hold is captured only when the primary check returns a definitive answer. "Company does not exist" counts as definitive and is billed. A bureau outage is not billed.
  • No delivery without a record. Results, billing, webhook and audit entry commit in one transaction (TX 2).
  • A wallet row is never locked during a bureau call. The hold is a single atomic UPDATE … WHERE balance - held + credit_limit >= amount, and the wallet_no_overspend CHECK constraint is the final guard.
  • Retries are safe. The idempotency key replays the stored response, and the capture is keyed capture:<hold_id> in the ledger.

1.4The waterfall router

Capabilities, not bureaus

Callers ask for a capability (company_registration, vat_status, tax_compliance_status, driver_licence, id_verification). Bureaus bundle data differently. Most return directors with the company enquiry, and PrDP usually comes with the licence record, so bundled data is modelled inside the parent capability's output. provider_capabilities holds the contracted wholesale price, volume tiers, any prepaid bundle balance, the timeout, and whether the provider is a live source (it queries CIPC or eNaTIS in real time) or serves a cached copy.

Scoring: expected cost, not list price

For every candidate provider:

score = marginal_price                              // 0 while a prepaid bundle has units (sunk cost)
      + 150c × (1 − success_rate)                   // a failure costs a retry and latency
      + 10c  × ewma_latency_seconds                 // user-facing latency has a price
      + 500c × (needs_live_data ∧ ¬is_live_source)  // new companies, freshness=live
  • success_rate is computed over the last 50 calls with a Beta(4,1) prior, so a new provider starts at an assumed 80% and is not starved of traffic.
  • marginal_price uses the volume tier reached by month-to-date volume, read from a Redis counter. Moving the marginal call to whichever bureau's tier break is closest is free margin.
  • The constants live in SCORING and are deliberately simple. Tune them from provider_calls, which holds the outcome, latency and cost of every attempt.

Execution rules

RuleWhy
Cost ceiling per request. The default company ceiling is R2.40, split 65/20/15% across registration, VAT and TCS.Stops a cascade of failures from turning a R3.00 sale into a R9.00 cost. When the budget is exhausted the result is unavailable, which triggers stale-if-error and no charge.
Overall deadline. Each attempt gets min(timeout, remaining).A sync API has a latency budget, and no attempt starts with less than 250 ms left.
Circuit breaker per provider and capability. Opens after 5 consecutive failures or ≥50% failures over 20 calls. Cooldown 30 s, doubling to 5 min; half-open sends one probe.State is shared through Redis, so all instances stop calling a dead bureau at once.
4xx does not count against health.A rejected request is our fault, not the bureau's.
Not-found confirmation. A "not found" from a cached-copy bureau, for a number captured this year or last year, is confirmed with a live source.Bureau copies of CIPC lag. Reporting "fake company" for a company registered last week is the worst possible false positive. CIPC Notice 1 of 2026 also confirms that the year in the number can precede the incorporation year.
Upstream-outage detection. If two or more providers fail on the same capability within 60 s, the capability is marked degraded.CIPC, eNaTIS and eFiling have their own outages, and every bureau that proxies them fails together. Without this rule a request would pay for a tour of every bureau. While degraded, requests go straight to stale-if-error, 10% of requests probe, and freshness=live bypasses the rule.

Adding a bureau

  1. Sign the contract and a POPIA s21 operator agreement, then record its date in providers.operator_agreement_signed_on.
  2. Add a BureauSpec in src/providers/adapters/httpBureau.ts with request() and map() per capability. Unit-test map() against anonymised recorded fixtures.
  3. Insert provider_capabilities rows with the contracted price, tiers and prepaid units, then add the code to ENABLED_PROVIDERS.
Placeholder mappings

The bureau endpoint paths and field names in httpBureau.ts are placeholders. Bureau APIs are contract-gated, and nothing here claims to reflect XDS, CPB, SearchWorks or VerifyID schemas. Confirm each bureau's product list before assuming it offers a capability. TCS and eNaTIS access in particular are not universal (see §1.7 and §2.8).

1.5POPIA-compliant caching

Scope is decided by the data

CapabilityScopeFreshStale-if-errorNot-found TTLRationale
company_registration (incl. directors)global7 days+30 days6 hPublic register (CIPC, Companies Act 71 of 2008). Reuse across buyers is compatible further processing (s15), and collection from a public record is permitted (s12(2)(a)).
vat_statusglobal7 days+30 days6 hSARS VAT vendor search is public.
tax_compliance_statustenant24 hnone1 hDisclosed by the taxpayer to one buyer via the PIN. A stale "compliant" is worse than "unknown".
driver_licencetenant24 hnone1 hA natural person's record, disclosed for one engagement.
id_verificationtenant30 daysnone1 hDHA data.

A vendor can choose to share its tenant-scoped results with another buyer through the Verified Vendor Passport (vendor_passport_shares). That is a separate flow based on the vendor's consent, with TshepoVet as the responsible party. It never happens through the cache.

Mechanics

  • Keys are HMAC(cache_key_key[scope], capability|subject). They are tenant-keyed for tenant scope, so a cache key for one tenant cannot be computed or matched for another.
  • Values are AES-256-GCM envelope-encrypted with AAD cache:<capability>:<scope>:<tenant>, so neither a Redis dump nor a DB snapshot exposes director ID numbers.
  • Freshness control. freshness: live bypasses the cache and is billed as COMPANY_CHECK_LIVE. max_age_seconds lets a buyer require recency without paying for live data. Every check reports source, data_as_of and stale.
  • Single-flight. Concurrent identical lookups share one bureau call: an in-process promise map plus a cross-instance Redis lock, with losers polling L1. A 5,000-row CSV upload containing the same supplier 40 times costs one bureau fee. The followers record zero cost.
  • Negative caching. Typos in bulk uploads do not trigger repeated paid lookups.
  • Purge. purge_after = stale_until, and run_retention_purge() deletes expired L2 rows. L1 TTLs equal the stale horizon.

At a 64% hit rate the check meets the 50% margin target.

With the defaults (R4.00 per miss, R0.05 overhead, 50% target): (1 − h) · 4.00 + 0.05 ≤ 1.50, so h ≥ 64%. How the product reaches that hit rate:

  1. The passport network effect. Supplier overlap between buyers in the same metro and sector is high, and every passport makes the next buyer's check a hit.
  2. Monitoring re-checks are cache-aware. Re-check at fresh_until, not on a per-tenant timer: one refresh serves all tenants.
  3. Prepaid bundles and tier routing. The router burns sunk-cost units first and pushes volume towards the next tier break.
  4. A COMPANY_CHECK_LIVE SKU at R6.00 makes buyers who demand freshness pay for it.

Two cost lines do not fit inside R3.00 and should be priced or bundled deliberately:

  • TCS verification. There is no public SARS API (see §2.8). If fulfilment is assisted (an eFiler checks the PIN) or bought from a bureau at a premium, either bundle TCS only into MONITORING_PER_VENDOR at R15/month or add a TCS surcharge. The code already separates the TCS cost budget (15% of the ceiling), so the effect on margin is visible.
  • LLM OCR on onboarding, roughly R0.50–R1.00 per document. That assumes about 3k input tokens (image, prompt and schema) and 1–2k output tokens including adaptive thinking, at Claude Opus 5.5 list prices of $4/$20 per MTok. Log usage per job and measure it. It is a one-off per vendor, amortised across every buyer through the passport. A cheaper first-pass model is a lever only if an eval on real SA documents shows field accuracy holds.

1.7Module notes

  • Continuous monitoring and accounting lock (R15/vendor/month). monitoring_enrolments uses an exclusion constraint, so a vendor can't be billed twice. expiry_schedules drives T-30, T-14, T-7 and T-0 reminders, then expire and lock after lock_grace_days. On lock, the ERP adapter applies the closest available control and emits vendor.payment_locked. Neither Xero nor Sage Business Cloud exposes a universal "payment block" flag through its API, so the realistic controls are: archive or tag the contact (Xero contact groups), set the supplier to inactive or on hold where the product supports it (Sage 200 Evolution and Sage Intacct have supplier hold settings), and run a pre-payment-batch check through the API. Document which control each connector uses, because buyers will ask whether the lock truly blocks payment.
  • B-BBEE scorecard (R5,000/month). Built on vendor_bbbee_profiles, with recognition levels and thresholds as data and the is_51pct_black_owned and is_30pct_black_female_owned generated columns. The Preferential Procurement indicators of the Amended Codes (TMPS-weighted spend with all Empowering Suppliers, QSEs, EMEs, ≥51% black-owned and ≥30% black-women-owned suppliers, plus the designated-group bonus) are aggregations over AP spend joined to these profiles. Sector-code thresholds must be loaded from the gazettes with verified=true before automatic classification is allowed.
  • COI and ghost-supplier radar (R3,500/month). HR identifiers are blind-indexed with a tenant-specific key (tenant_employees). The worker projects vendor director IDs, bank accounts, phone numbers and addresses into coi_vendor_index with the same tenant key, so matching is a tenant-local join and identifiers cannot be linked across tenants (see the s57 analysis in §2.6). Fuzzy name plus address matching uses pg_trgm.
  • Industry add-ons. CIDB grading (grade 1–9 plus class of works, e.g. 7GB PE) and COIDA Letters of Good Standing live in vendor_credentials. In construction, letters of good standing can come from the Compensation Fund or a licensed mutual such as FEM, so the issuer is stored. Driving licences and PrDP use the same table, and the logistics add-on gates the required_codes and required_prdp workflows.

1.9Production hardening still to do

  • Async verifications run in-process. A crash mid-flight is covered (holds expire after 15 minutes and the purge job marks the verification unable_to_verify), but production should move process() onto a durable queue (SQS or BullMQ) with the existing function as the handler.
  • KMS provider. LocalKeyProvider is for development and test. Implement KeyProvider with AWS KMS GenerateDataKey and Decrypt, plus DEK caching.
  • Partitioning. audit_log is partitioned monthly. Partition provider_calls and verification_checks the same way once they pass about 50M rows.
  • Secure TCS link endpoint and WhatsApp Flow handler. Designed in WhatsApp Onboarding §5 but not implemented.

POPIA, SARS and FICA compliance workflow

Section references are to the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) unless stated otherwise.

Not legal advice

This is an engineering-grade compliance design. Have South African privacy counsel review the roles matrix (§2.1), the s57 assessment (§2.6) and the privacy notice wording before launch.

2.1Who is who: roles decide obligations

POPIA protects juristic persons as well as natural persons (s1, "data subject"), so a supplier company's own details are personal information, not just its directors' details. TshepoVet plays two different roles:

2.3Consent capture, step by step

A. Inline API attestation (the buyer captured consent in its own process)

  1. Notice. The buyer shows the data subject the TshepoVet-provided notice text (company_verification or driver_verification). Notices are versioned and immutable, and body_sha256 is the hash of the exact text.
  2. Capture. The buyer records the subject's agreement (signed form, e-signature, portal checkbox) and keeps the evidence.
  3. Attest. The API request carries consent: {notice_code, subject_display, subject_role, on_behalf_of_subject, purposes[], captured_at, evidence_reference, attested_by}.
  4. Record. recordAttestation() resolves the active notice version and stores a consent_records row with capture_method = 'api_attestation', the evidence, the API key that attested, and evidence_sha256 = sha256(canonical(evidence) ‖ notice_hash). That hash is tamper evidence over both what was agreed and the text agreed to.
  5. Granular purposes. One consent_purposes row per purpose, each with its own lawful basis. Purposes can be withdrawn individually.
  6. Assert. Before reserving funds, assertPurposes() checks that the consent belongs to the tenant, has not expired, and covers every purpose the request needs. If not, the response is 422 with missing_purposes, and nothing is charged or created.
  7. Audit. verification.created is logged with the consent_id in the hash-chained audit log.

B. WhatsApp (TshepoVet captures consent directly)

  1. The buyer invites the vendor, which creates a conversation in the INVITED state.
  2. The first message is the short notice with three buttons: I agree, More info, No thanks. "More info" shows the full s18 elements: responsible party, purpose, whether supply is voluntary, consequences, recipients, cross-border transfers, rights and the Information Regulator.
  3. Only the button tap counts. Free text such as "yes ok" re-prompts. Consent must be a "voluntary, specific and informed expression of will" (s1), and a structured tap is unambiguous evidence.
  4. The worker records capture_method = 'whatsapp_button' with the WhatsApp message id, a masked phone number, the timestamp, the purposes and the notice hash.
  5. STOP (or YEKA or HOU OP) in any state withdraws every purpose and moves the conversation to OPTED_OUT. This is the s11(3) objection path.

C. What withdrawal does

  • A future check that needs the purpose fails with 422.
  • Monitoring stops: the monitoring period ends, the TCS PIN is marked revoked and crypto-shredded on the next purge run, and expiry schedules are cancelled.
  • Results already delivered stay with the buyer under its own retention basis. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it (s11(2)(b)).

2.5Automated decisions

Under s71 a data subject may not be subject to a decision with legal consequences based solely on automated processing that profiles reliability or creditworthiness, unless appropriate measures apply. Supplier onboarding outcomes can amount to such a decision, so:

  • outcome and risk_flags are decision support. The API never rejects a vendor; the buyer decides.
  • OCR issues involving a person (ID mismatch, possible tampering) produce needs_review, a human queue, never an automatic rejection.
  • Vendors can make representations through the WhatsApp HELP/AGENT path and the portal correction flow.
  • Every flag is a stable, documented code with its source and data_as_of. That is the "sufficient information about the underlying logic" in s71(3)(b).

2.7Cross-border transfers

  • The default region is af-south-1 for compute, Postgres, Redis, S3 and KMS.
  • LLM OCR is a transfer under s72 if the API endpoint is offshore. Basis: s72(1)(b), a binding agreement with adequate protection (DPA, zero-data-retention terms where available), with s72(1)(a) consent as a second basis. Minimise by cropping to the document, stripping EXIF metadata, and sending one document per request.
  • Logs and APM are often offshore SaaS, which is why the code logs references rather than values and pino redacts known paths.
  • Bureaus are South African. Confirm in each contract where their processing and backups sit.

2.8SARS TCS PIN handling

How TCS works, and the edge cases that matter

  • Since 2016 SARS has issued a Tax Compliance Status PIN on eFiling instead of paper tax clearance certificates (Tax Administration Act s256). The taxpayer requests a TCS (Good Standing, Tender, Foreign Investment Allowance or Emigration) and shares the PIN.
  • A third party verifies the PIN on eFiling, using an eFiling profile, together with the taxpayer's tax reference number. The result shows the status at the moment of the check. A PIN that showed compliant yesterday can show non-compliant today if a return becomes overdue or a debt arises.
  • The taxpayer chooses the expiry date when requesting the TCS, typically up to 12 months. Read it from the result; never assume 12 months.
  • The taxpayer can cancel a PIN at any time, which surfaces as pin_status: revoked.
No public SARS API

SARS publishes no third-party TCS API. That leaves three fulfilment channels: (1) a bureau or partner with a lawful channel, verified contractually, with SARS's position in writing; (2) assisted eFiling verification, where a TshepoVet operator acting as an authorised eFiler checks PINs in a queue; (3) the National Treasury CSD report for suppliers on the Central Supplier Database. Do not automate eFiling by screen-scraping without SARS's written approval: it breaches eFiling terms and puts the whole channel at risk. The engine models TCS as an ordinary capability with a long timeout, so any channel can be plugged in without changing callers.

PIN lifecycle

  1. Collect over a channel that doesn't leave the PIN lying around. In WhatsApp, send a single-use 30-minute link or a WhatsApp Flow (encrypted end to end to our endpoint), so the PIN isn't in chat history. If the vendor types it in chat anyway, accept it (it is their own history), store it encrypted in Redis with a TTL, and tell them how to avoid it next time.
  2. Normalise and sanity-check. Strip spaces and dashes, uppercase, and require 6–20 alphanumerics. A 10-digit value starting 0–4 or 9 is probably the tax or VAT number pasted by mistake, so ask the vendor to confirm. The API rejects a PIN equal to the tax reference.
  3. Never log or echo it. It does not appear in responses, webhooks, idempotency records or audit details (only a mask showing the last two characters). The integration tests assert this.
  4. Fingerprint it with a tenant-keyed HMAC. The fingerprint deduplicates PINs and is part of the tenant-scoped cache key, so the plaintext is never a lookup key.
  5. Verify, then store the result: TCS status, PIN status, expiry, check time, and whether the taxpayer name matches CIPC (tcs_taxpayer_name_mismatch).
  6. Store the PIN only if the buyer enrolled the vendor in monitoring and the PIN is active. It is envelope-encrypted, bound to tenant and vendor, records the key version for rotation, and expires with the PIN. Each decryption for a re-check writes an audit event.
  7. Re-check on a schedule and before payment runs. A move to non-compliant fires vendor.compliance_changed and, after the grace period, vendor.payment_locked.
  8. Expiry. Reminders go out at T-30, T-14 and T-7, using a WhatsApp utility template if the 24-hour window has closed. At expiry the status becomes unknown, never "compliant", and the vendor is asked for a new PIN.
  9. Purge. At expiry, revocation, withdrawal of consent or the end of monitoring, the purge job deletes the encrypted PIN. The result history stays as due-diligence evidence.

2.9Auto-purge of expired personal information

  • Retention clocks run from creation, the end of the relationship, expiry or last activity. Recording the end of a buyer-vendor relationship starts the five-year clocks for due-diligence evidence.
  • Statutory holds override purges. A buyer that is a FICA accountable institution keeps customer due diligence records for five years after the relationship ends (FIC Act s22–23). Tax records follow the Tax Administration Act s29 and company records the Companies Act s24. Configure these per tenant and never shorten them below the statutory minimum.
  • Litigation or regulator hold: add a per-vendor hold date that the purge function respects (a recommended extension).
  • Backups are handled by crypto-shredding. Live ciphertext is removed, and backup ciphertext becomes unrecoverable when the key version that wrapped it is retired after the backup window. Document this in the PAIA manual.

2.11FICA, B-BBEE and entity edge cases

FICA, when the buyer is an accountable institution

  • Beneficial ownership: collect a declaration for natural persons holding 5% or more or exercising control. CIPC's beneficial ownership register (since the General Laws Amendment Act 22 of 2022) is not public, so rely on the vendor's declaration with a supporting share register.
  • Sanctions and PEPs: screen against the Targeted Financial Sanctions list (FIC Act s26A–C) and domestic PEPs/DPIPs by adding a sanctions_screening capability to the waterfall. The engine is generic.
  • Keep customer due diligence records for five years after the relationship ends.

  • Sector codes differ (Construction, Tourism, ICT, Financial, AgriBEE, Transport, MAC, Property, Forestry, Defence). Thresholds are data rows. Load them from the gazettes and mark them verified only after review; until then, vendors in those sectors go to manual review.
  • Validity is 12 months from the commissioning or issue date. Affidavits must be on the dtic template, deposed by a director or member, and commissioned by a Commissioner of Oaths. A missing commissioner block is a hard fail.
  • EMEs automatically qualify as Empowering Suppliers. For others, the certificate must state it.
  • Misrepresenting B-BBEE status is an offence (B-BBEE Act s13O), so suspicious affidavits (ownership inconsistent with the CIPC director list, turnover sitting on a threshold) go to review rather than being accepted silently.

Entity edge cases

  • Sole proprietors are not on CIPC. The anchor is the owner's SA ID (DHA verification), with the income tax reference and bank account verification. There is a dedicated resolution function for them.
  • Close corporations have members, not directors, and no new CCs have been registered since 1 May 2011, so a CC suffix on a later number is flagged.
  • Trusts (Master's Office IT numbers) and unincorporated NPOs (DSD numbers) go to a human in the WhatsApp flow in v1.
  • Public-sector buyers need a CSD number (MAAA plus 7 digits, checked by a database constraint) for every supplier.
